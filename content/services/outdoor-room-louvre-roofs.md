    - q: "What's the difference between SunPivot and SunFold?"
      a: "Both have aluminium blades that tilt to control sun and shade. SunFold blades can also retract and stack to one side, opening up to 75% of the roof to the sky."
    - q: "What do the rain and wind sensors do?"
      a: "A rain sensor closes the louvre roof automatically when it starts to rain, so the area stays dry even when you're not home. A wind sensor rolls the drop-down blinds back up when the wind gets too strong. We help you choose the settings."
    - q: "Can I control it with a remote?"
      a: "Yes. One remote can run the louvre roof, the outdoor blinds and the lighting."
    - q: "Can you turn it into a full outdoor room?"
      a: "Yes. We add Glaslide glass sliding doors, sliding aluminium louvre screens or Solarzip outdoor blinds to close in the sides."
    - q: "Does it have to attach to my house?"
      a: "No. Bask louvre roofs can be wall-mounted, freestanding, or built into the house. Most homes can take a wall-mounted roof, fixed to a large lintel above the windows or to the studs behind weatherboard or sheet cladding. Brick homes may need support from the roof structure, which we talk through when we measure."
    - q: "How big can a louvre roof be?"
      a: "Almost any size. Larger areas are covered with several louvre roof sections joined together, and each section can be opened and closed on its own. The largest size for a single section is set by the NZ Building Code standards."
    - q: "Can it be made to suit my house?"
      a: "Yes. Every Bask louvre roof is custom-made to fit your space and your home."
    - q: "Where does the rainwater go?"
      a: "Into a gutter built into the frame, then down pipes hidden inside the aluminium legs and out through an outlet at the bottom. You choose which legs carry the downpipes, usually the one closest to your stormwater drain."
    - q: "Can I collect the rainwater?"
      a: "Yes. The downpipes can run into a water tank. The powder-coated aluminium needs little cleaning, so the water is fairly clean, but it must be treated before you drink it."

A deck is only as good as the weather lets it be. An adjustable louvre roof
changes that — open it up for full sun, angle it for shade, or close it flat
in the rain, all from a remote or a rain sensor.

Paired with glass or aluminium walls, it becomes a genuine outdoor room:
somewhere to use whatever Northland's weather is doing.

We supply Bask louvre and outdoor products, so the roof itself is backed by a proven
system — we handle the measuring, structure and installation around it.
