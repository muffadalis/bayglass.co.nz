Want shelter without opening blades? The Bask VelaSun is a fixed aluminium
pergola roof that keeps the sun and rain off your deck or patio, so you can use
it all year round.

The frame is solid aluminium, low maintenance and built to last, powder coated
to blend with your home. Each VelaSun is custom designed and made in New Zealand
to suit your space, whether it's a new build or an existing home.
