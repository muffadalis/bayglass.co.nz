### Local glaziers, based in Waipapa

We're Bay Glass Kerikeri, working out of our workshop at 40 Klinac Lane in Waipapa. We've been a family-run business for 37 years, cutting, fitting and fixing glass for homes and businesses across Northland — Kerikeri, Paihia, Russell, Kawakawa, Kaikohe, Haruru, Opua and right through the Far North. We're now under new management, keeping the same local service and making it even better.

Our team of three glaziers does the work themselves, from the first measure to the final clean-up. You deal with the same local people the whole way through.

### If it has glass, we can make it

Most of our work starts with a phone call about one job and turns into a long relationship. We look after:

- [Glass repairs, new glazing and mirrors](/services/glass-glazing/) — broken windows replaced same-day where possible during working hours, with board-ups when glass can't go in straight away
- [Retrofit double glazing](/services/retrofit-double-glazing/) — warmer, quieter rooms without replacing your joinery
- [Frameless glass showers](/services/glass-showers/) and [splashbacks](/services/splashbacks/)
- [Balustrades and pool fences](/services/balustrades-pool-fences/)
- [Security and insect screens](/services/security-insect-screens/)
- [Outdoor rooms, louvre roofs](/services/outdoor-room-louvre-roofs/) and [pergolas](/services/pergola-roof/) — we're an authorised dealer for Bask Outdoor Systems
- Commercial glazing

### How we work

Tell us what you need and we'll come and measure. Quotes are free and there's no obligation, and we usually have yours back within two to three working days. Once you're happy, we make it to size and install it ourselves.

All the glass we install is safety glass where the Building Code requires it, fitted to NZS 4223. Every job comes with a one-year workmanship warranty.
